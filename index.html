Future Vision

Integration Roadmap:
Neuralink & Real EEG Systems

NeuroBridge is built as a software layer designed to interface directly with next-generation BCI hardware — including implantable and non-invasive systems.

🔌
Neuralink N1 Chip Integration
NeuroBridge's inference API is designed to accept raw electrode streams from implantable devices like Neuralink's N1 chip, enabling 1024-channel neural decoding at surgical precision.
🧢
Consumer EEG Headsets
Compatible with OpenBCI, Emotiv EPOC+, and Muse S. NeuroBridge's edge model runs on 14–64 channel consumer hardware with on-device neural processing at under 50ms latency.
🤖
Motor Cortex Prosthetic Control
Future integration with robotic prosthetics and exoskeletons — translating decoded motor imagery signals into precise limb movement control for spinal cord injury patients.
